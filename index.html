Find duplicate photos

Choose a folder or a few photos. DupeLens compares them in this browser, groups copies and look-alikes, and lets you decide which ones to keep.

You can also drop photos or folders anywhere on this page.

The sample photos are drawn by DupeLens in this browser: a few pictures plus resized, edited and copied versions of them.

  1. 1

    Choose photos

    Pick a folder or single photos. They are read with the browser's File API and analysed in this page.

  2. 2

    Review groups

    Photos that look alike are shown together. DupeLens suggests which one to keep; you can change every decision.

  3. 3

    Export

    Download your decisions as a list, or a script that moves the photos you marked into a review folder.

    Help

    How it works

    1. Choose photos — a folder, single photos, or the sample photos. They are read with the browser's File API and analysed in this page.
    2. Review groups — photos that look alike are grouped. Keep the suggestion or switch Keep / Remove, then accept the group.
    3. Export — download your decisions (JSON, CSV) or a move script. DupeLens itself does not write to your files.

    What the similarity means

    SimilarityTypical cases
    100 %Exact copies: the same file, or the same pixels saved again
    95 %Resized or re-saved copies and very light edits
    90 % (default)Also brightness or colour edits, small crops, an added caption
    80 %Also bigger crops, black-and-white versions, strong edits
